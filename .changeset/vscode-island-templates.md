An island's template is checked in the editor as the server shell it is: a fence binding misused in it, a misspelled `use()` field, and a `read()` fallback of the wrong type are underlined where they are written. `this.attrs` is typed from `static attrs`, and an island that declares its shell's props with `Stator.props<P>()` in the fence has them typed in the template.
